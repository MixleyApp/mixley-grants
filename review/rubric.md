# Review Rubric

Each application is scored against five criteria. The rubric is public so applicants know what is evaluated.

## Criteria

| Criterion | Weight | What we look for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Concreteness | 20% | A specific contribution, not a vague idea. The proposal names the component it touches and the deliverable. |
| Public body of work | 20% | A repository, a published integration, or a written brief that supports the proposal. |
| Feasibility | 20% | The scope is achievable in the stated timeframe by the applicant. |
| Alignment | 20% | The contribution advances Mixley rather than only the applicant's own project. |
| Licensing | 20% | The work is released under MIT or a compatible license, or the applicant states the exception in the proposal. |

## Scoring

- **Approved**: total at least 70% and no criterion below 50%.
- **Revision requested**: total at least 50% and at least one criterion between 50% and 70%.
- **Declined**: total below 50%, or any criterion below 50% that cannot be revised.

## Settlement

- Micro and Standard tiers are settled in credits through the credits ledger.
- Major tier is recorded as a credit-equivalent commitment and settled in MIX at token launch.

Reviewers record the decision and the award in the application issue.
